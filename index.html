NextGenPH — Youth Innovators Reimagining Public Service

NextGenPH is an open and participatory innovation contest that invites youth to design and pitch practical solutions to improve public services. The initiative aims to foster faster, more responsive public service delivery — a platform that values creativity, collaboration, and public impact. Ideas submitted should be grounded in personal experience or informed by the experiences of others in availing public services.

About NextGenPH

The Development Academy of the Philippines, Center of Excellence on Public-Sector Productivity (DAP COE-PSP) recognizes innovation's crucial role in improving public services and productivity.

NextGenPH is an open and participatory innovation contest that invites youth to share their collective ideas and visions for how government services should be experienced by the public. This initiative seeks to capture a wide range of perspectives to inspire more citizen-centered, accessible, and effective public service delivery.

This contest features two categories and is open to both Senior High School and College students.
